# Product Roadmap

1. [ ] **Ticket Submission Form** — User-friendly form with guided fields, environment detection, and validation for complete bug reports `[S]`
2. [ ] **Ticket Database & Basic CRUD** — Centralized storage for tickets with create, read, update, delete operations and basic search `[S]`
3. [ ] **Ticket Status Tracking** — Full lifecycle tracking from submission through verification, assignment, in-progress, and resolution states `[S]`
4. [ ] **Admin Kanban Board** — Visual ticket management with drag-and-drop workflow states for efficient prioritization `[M]`
5. [ ] **Ticket Filtering & Search** — Powerful filtering by status, severity, priority, assignee, and labels to find relevant tickets quickly `[S]`
6. [ ] **Bulk Actions** — Batch operations to assign, update status, or tag multiple tickets simultaneously `[M]`
7. [ ] **User Portal & Ticket Tracking** — Dashboard where users can view their submitted tickets, track status, and access resolution summaries `[M]`
8. [ ] **Notifications System** — Automatic email and in-app notifications when ticket status changes, with user-configurable preferences `[L]`
9. [ ] **Analytics & Reports** — Charts and metrics showing ticket distribution by status, severity levels, submission trends, and resolution times `[M]`
10. [ ] **PDF Export** — Generate and download reports for archival or sharing with stakeholders `[S]`

> Notes
> - Order items by technical dependencies and product architecture
> - Each item should represent an end-to-end (frontend + backend) functional and testable feature
