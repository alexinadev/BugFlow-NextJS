# Product Mission

## Problem

Chaotic bug reporting through email and chat creates confusion, lost information, and no visibility into resolution status. Support teams struggle to track, prioritize, and close feedback loops with end-users.

## Target Users

- **End-Users:** Software users who need to report bugs and track their resolution status
- **Administrators:** Support teams managing tickets, assigning work, and communicating updates

## Solution

BugFlow is a streamlined ticketing system that transforms unstructured bug reports into organized, trackable tickets with complete visibility. It provides:

- **For Users:** Simple guided submission forms and a portal to view ticket status and resolutions
- **For Admins:** A centralized dashboard with Kanban and list views to manage the entire ticket lifecycle
- **For Everyone:** Transparent status updates and automated notifications that close the feedback loop

The focus is on simplicity, transparency, and keeping support teams and users aligned throughout the resolution process.
